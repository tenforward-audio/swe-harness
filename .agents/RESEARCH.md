# Research findings

This file preserves planning and research outcomes that are worth keeping after
the related idea or local workspace is deliberately abandoned. It is a durable
knowledge archive, not an intake queue, status board, or substitute for current
project documentation.

Record only findings likely to prevent repeated investigation or materially
inform later work. Put a public project claim in the owning documentation and a
consequential architectural choice in an ADR instead. Do not duplicate active
card state, a planning question, or its durable ledger resolution here.

## Record shape

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD — Short subject

- Question: What the investigation tried to establish.
- Finding: What was learned, including important limitations or uncertainty.
- Evidence: Observed sources, checks, or prototypes; label anything unobserved.
- Outcome: Why the idea was abandoned or deferred.
- Revisit when: A concrete trigger, or "No trigger identified."
```
